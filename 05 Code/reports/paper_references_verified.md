# Verified References for the IEEE Paper

Verification date: 4 October 2026. Every reference cited in `paper/ieee_paper.docx` is listed here in citation order.
Journal and conference metadata (authors, title, journal, volume, issue, pages, year and DOI) were checked against the
Crossref DOI record (`api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`). Abstracts were read where the claim in the paper goes beyond the
title (Semantic Scholar abstract API or the publisher page). Official datasets and government documents were checked
against the copies stored in `data/raw/` and the URLs in `data/raw/source_manifest.csv`.

Claims marked *title-level* are deliberately limited to what the title states, because the full text was not read.

**Removed during verification:** J. Hinkel (2011), *Global Environmental Change* 21(1):198–208. The metadata are
correct, but its abstract could not be accessed to confirm the specific argument the draft attributed to it, so the
sentence and the citation were removed.
